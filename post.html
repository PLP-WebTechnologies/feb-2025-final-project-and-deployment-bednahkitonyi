Building Accessible Web Interfaces

April 25, 2025 • 6 min read

Accessibility ensures that digital content is usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Implementing accessibility from the ground up not only broadens your audience but also improves SEO and user experience.

Why Accessibility Matters

According to the World Health Organization, over 1 billion people live with some form of disability. Ignoring accessibility is ignoring a huge portion of users who rely on keyboard navigation, screen readers, or other assistive technologies.

Common Practices

  • Use semantic HTML elements like <header>, <nav>, and <main>.
  • Ensure sufficient color contrast between text and background.
  • Provide meaningful alt text for images.
  • Make forms navigable and labeled properly.

Getting Started

Start small: test your site with just a keyboard. Use tools like Lighthouse or WAVE to audit accessibility. Make it a habit to review these checks regularly in your dev workflow.
